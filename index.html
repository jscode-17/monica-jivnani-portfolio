About

Founder. Builder.
Author.

I'm Monica Jivnani, a first-generation entrepreneur who started from nothing and built MaMo TechnoLabs LLP, a CMMI Level 3 global IT company, from the ground up in 2014. What began as a quiet interest in technology turned into 15 years of hands-on experience across software development, product strategy, international account management, and business leadership.

Today I work with clients across 25+ countries, independently managing 20+ international accounts every year. I specialize in turning complex business problems into working digital solutions on time, within scope, and built to last.

I'm also a published author of three books, a public speaker, and someone who genuinely enjoys the problem-solving side of both technology and storytelling.

Expertise

My key areas of expertise.

I've worked at every level of the technology stack, from writing code to leading delivery teams to managing global client relationships. These four areas are where my experience runs deepest and where I can make the most practical difference for your business.

Strategy & Storytelling

I help businesses build clear IT roadmaps, cloud strategies, and digital transformation plans that are grounded in real business goals, not just technology trends. As a published author, I also bring that same discipline to brand communication and leadership messaging, helping companies say what they actually mean in a way people understand and trust.

Product Development & IT Consulting

I've guided products from early concept to working MVP and well beyond across SaaS platforms, custom web and mobile applications, cloud environments, and enterprise system integrations. My consulting approach is direct: understand what needs to be built first, avoid scope creep, and ship something that holds up under real-world pressure.

International Account & Partnership Management

I manage 20+ international client accounts every year, across Europe, North America, the Middle East, and beyond. Published Author & Brand Storytelling
