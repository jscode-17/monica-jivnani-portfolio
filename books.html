Books By Monica Jivnani

The Last Bloom Before The Fall

About the last beautiful things that appear before something ends: a relationship, a season, a version of yourself you're about to leave behind. If you've ever held onto something while knowing it was changing, this book will feel familiar. It's written with care and a lot of close attention to how people actually feel in those moments.

Arjuna and the Women Who Waited

This is a story about what it means to be waiting for and what it costs the people doing the waiting. Arjun moves through a world shaped by the women around him, each of them carrying something he doesn't fully see. It's a quiet book, but it has weight. The kind of story that stays with you after you put it down.

Blindfolded: The Queen Who Chose Darkness

A tighter, faster story than my first two. About decisions made without the full picture and how people live with what follows. Less quiet, more urgent, the kind of book that keeps you reading past the time you planned to stop.

About Monica


Monica is an author and storyteller whose work reimagines epic narratives through the voices of women, silence, and emotional depth. Drawing inspiration from the Mahabharata, her writing explores love, sacrifice, dignity, and the unseen strength that shapes destinies. Her books blend lyrical prose with timeless myth to create intimate, reflective storytelling.
